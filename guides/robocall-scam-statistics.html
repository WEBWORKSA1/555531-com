The YouMail Robocall Index, which extrapolates from calls to its app users, put US robocall volume at about 52.5 billion in 2025 — essentially flat on 2024 and within the 50–55 billion range seen for five years. Monthly figures swung between roughly 3.8 and 5 billion. Scam and telemarketing calls made up the majority of volume most months.

Fraud losses

Consumers reported a record $15.9 billion in fraud losses to the FTC’s Consumer Sentinel Network for 2025, up from $12.5 billion in 2024, across roughly three million fraud reports. Investment scams accounted for the largest share of losses. The FBI’s Internet Crime Complaint Center separately reported about $20.9 billion in internet-crime losses for 2025.

The FTC has stressed that reported losses are only a fraction of the true total; for 2024 it estimated overall consumer fraud costs could be as high as $195.9 billion once under-reporting is considered.

The phone’s special danger

Texts and social media now reach more people than phone calls, and social media produces the largest total losses. But scams that start with a phone call produce the highest typical loss per victim. The FTC’s reporting on older consumers found median losses for phone-initiated scams in the low thousands of dollars — several times higher than for scams that start on social media. A live voice applies pressure that a text can’t.

AI’s growing role

  • A Gallup and Stop Scams Alliance survey (2026) estimated about 12% of successful scams in 2025 involved AI or deepfakes, within an estimated $68 billion in US scam losses.
  • The FBI’s IC3 logged more than 22,000 complaints it classified as AI-related in 2025, the first year it tracked the category.
  • McAfee found about one in four adults had experienced, or knew someone who had experienced, a voice-clone scam.

The business side

As consumers screen calls, legitimate businesses struggle to get answered — one reason branded caller ID and call authentication are growing. Global VoIP services were estimated at about $160 billion in 2025, forecast to reach roughly $340 billion by 2032.

How to read these numbers

  • Robocall indices are modelled estimates, not carrier counts.
  • Fraud figures are self-reported and skew toward people who know where to report.
  • Years differ: always check which period a statistic describes.
