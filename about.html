About

A calmer phone for everyone

555531 — The Number Desk is an independent publisher of free phone-safety tools and plain-English guides, based in Montréal, Canada and written for readers across North America and beyond.

Why we exist

The phone used to be the trusted channel. Now it carries tens of billions of robocalls a year, spoofed caller IDs, AI-cloned voices and some of the costliest scams per victim of any channel. Most advice online is either buried in government PDFs or pushed by apps that want a subscription. We sit in between: practical tools that work instantly in your browser, guides that respect your time, and no fear-selling.

What we build

  • Tools — the Number Inspector, Scam Call Checker, 31-Day Quiet Line, Area Code Directory, Fictional Number Generator and VoIP calculator.
  • Guides — researched articles that cite regulators and primary data.
  • Community — the monthly Scam Spotter Challenge and The 31 Report newsletter.
  • Business Phone Desk — independent help choosing a phone system.

How we’re funded

Display advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships, referral fees when a business chooses a provider we introduced, and reader support. None of these influence tool results or editorial conclusions — see our editorial policy.

What we are not

We are not a government agency, carrier or law-enforcement body, and we never phone anyone. We don’t sell personal data or run people-search lookups on private individuals.
