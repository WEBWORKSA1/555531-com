Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026.

Summary

Our tools run in your browser — phone numbers you inspect are not sent to us. We collect personal information only when you submit a form. We don’t sell personal information.

Information we collect

  • Form submissions (contact, quotes, kit, newsletter, contest, careers, donations, advertising): the details you enter, the page you submitted from, and basic attribution (referrer and campaign parameters).
  • Browser storage: theme, cookie choice, recent lookups, 31-day checklist progress. This stays on your device; you can clear it any time.
  • Technical data collected by our host (GitHub Pages) and advertising partners, such as IP address, browser type and pages visited.

How we use it

To answer you, prepare quote shortlists you request, deliver the kit and newsletter you ask for, run the contest, consider job applications, and improve the site. If you request business quotes, we share your request only with the providers you approve.

Form processing

Forms are transmitted securely through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards the content to our inbox. Please don’t send passwords, card numbers or government ID numbers through any form.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info. Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Until you choose “Accept all” in our cookie banner, we signal Google Consent Mode “denied” for ad storage and personalisation, and request non-personalised ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google’s certified consent tools may also be shown. See our Cookie Policy.

Embedded content

Videos use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. Google Fonts are loaded from Google’s servers.

Retention

Inquiries and leads: up to 24 months. Contest entries: 12 months after the round. Talent-pool applications: 12 months. Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Quebec Law 25, PIPEDA, or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may request access, correction, deletion or portability of your data, withdraw consent, or opt out of “sale” or “sharing”. We don’t sell personal information. Use the contact form and choose “Privacy request”.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 (16 in the EEA), and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will post updates here with a new date.
