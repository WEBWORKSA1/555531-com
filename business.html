What a modern plan usually includes

  • Unlimited domestic calling and business texting
  • Desktop and mobile apps — your business number on your own phone
  • Auto-attendant (“press 1 for sales”), ring groups and call queues
  • Voicemail-to-email and transcription
  • Call recording and AI call summaries
  • CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Teams)
  • Number porting — keep the number customers already know

Who needs more than a basic plan?

Teams answering high call volumes need contact-centre features (queues, wallboards, IVR, QA). Regulated industries need HIPAA, PCI or call-recording retention. Ask for these specifically when you request quotes.

Choose your number

Local, toll-free or vanity?

📍

Local

Signals you are nearby. Best for trades, clinics, restaurants and anyone who wants call-back rates to stay high.

🆓

Toll-free

800, 833, 844, 855, 866, 877, 888. National reach and a bigger-company feel; you pay for inbound minutes.

🔤

Vanity

Spells a word (1-800-FLOWERS style) or a memorable pattern. Great for radio, billboards and vehicles.

🔁

Port your own

Keep your existing number when you switch. Never cancel the old service before the port completes.

Will customers answer? Branded caller ID and STIR/SHAKEN

Because robocalls are everywhere, many people no longer pick up unknown numbers — and carriers label suspicious traffic as “Spam Likely”. Two things help legitimate businesses get answered:

  • STIR/SHAKEN attestation. North American carriers digitally sign calls to show the caller is allowed to use the number. Ask providers whether your outbound calls receive full (“A”) attestation.
  • Branded caller ID. Registered business name, logo and call reason on the recipient’s screen. Answer rates typically rise sharply versus an unlabelled number.
  • Register for texting. US business texting on local numbers requires 10DLC brand and campaign registration; unregistered traffic gets filtered.

Buyer checklist

  1. Count real users, not desks — include part-timers who need the app.
  2. List must-haves: call recording, queues, SMS, fax, integrations, compliance.
  3. Ask for total monthly cost including taxes and regulatory fees.
  4. Check contract length and early-termination terms.
  5. Confirm the porting timeline in writing.
  6. Test call quality on your own internet connection during the trial.
  7. Ask about 911/E911 address handling for remote staff.
