FAQ

Quick answers

Can this site tell me exactly who called me?

No website can reliably name a private caller from a number alone, and caller ID can be faked. The Number Inspector tells you what a number is — country, region, line type, formats and structural red flags — and the Scam Check tells you whether the call behaved like a scam. That combination is what actually protects you.

Why does the do-not-call registry take 31 days?

Covered telemarketers in the US and Canada have up to 31 days after you register to update their call lists. The 31-Day Quiet Line tool calculates your date and gives you a calendar reminder.

Are 555 phone numbers real?

Only 555-0100 through 555-0199 are reserved for fiction in North America, and 555-1212 is directory assistance. Any other 555 number is not guaranteed to be fake. Need safe numbers? Use our generator.

Do you store the numbers I look up?

No. Lookups run in your browser. Your last few lookups are saved only on your own device so you can revisit them, and you can clear them anytime.
