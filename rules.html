Official rules

Scam Spotter Challenge — a free, skill-based monthly contest. No purchase or payment of any kind is necessary to enter or win.

1. Organiser

The contest is run by 555531.com (“we”). Contact us through the contact page.

2. Eligibility

Open to individuals who are at least 18 years old (or the age of majority where they live) in jurisdictions where skill contests are lawful. Void where prohibited or restricted. Our team, contractors, sponsors and their immediate families are not eligible.

3. Entry period

Each monthly round opens on the 1st and closes at 23:59 (Eastern Time) on the last day of the month. Entries received after the close roll into the next round.

4. How to enter

Complete the quiz and submit the entry form with your name, email, country, quiz score and a written tip of up to 600 characters. One entry per person per round. Automated, bulk or third-party entries are void.

5. Judging

Entries scoring 7/10 or higher qualify. A panel scores qualifying tips on accuracy (50%), clarity (30%) and originality (20%). Ties are resolved by random draw. Judges’ decisions are final. Canadian winners must correctly answer a time-limited mathematical skill-testing question.

6. Prizes

When a prize pool is funded for a round, it will be announced on the challenge page before entries close — for example one grand prize (up to US$555) and three runner-up prizes (US$31 each), delivered as a digital gift card or, at the winner’s choice, a donation to a registered consumer-protection charity. Prizes are not transferable. Winners are responsible for any taxes. If a round has no funded pool, winners receive recognition in the Hall of Spotters.

7. Notification

Winners are contacted by email within 14 days of the close and must respond within 14 days, or an alternate winner may be selected. We will never ask a winner to pay any fee, share banking passwords or buy gift cards to claim a prize — anyone who does is a scammer.

8. Publicity and content

By entering you confirm your tip is your own original work and grant us a non-exclusive licence to publish it with your first name and country. You may opt out of publicity by telling us when you are notified.

9. Privacy

Entry data is used only to run the contest and, if you opt in, to send The 31 Report. See our Privacy Policy.

10. General

We may cancel, suspend or modify a round if fraud, technical failure or any other factor impairs its integrity. These rules are governed by the laws of the province of Quebec, Canada, subject to mandatory local consumer law.
