What exactly happens in the 31 days

In the United States, telemarketers covered by the Telemarketing Sales Rule must download the National Do Not Call Registry at least every 31 days and remove registered numbers. Since 2005 they have had up to 31 days from your registration date to stop calling you. Canada’s National Do Not Call List gives telemarketers the same 31-day window. After that, a sales call from a covered company is a potential violation you can report.

Calls the registry won’t stop

  • Scam calls — criminals ignore the list entirely, which is why blocking matters.
  • Political calls, charities and surveys (rules vary by country).
  • Debt collectors and informational calls (appointment reminders, delivery notices).
  • Companies you have an existing business relationship with — unless you ask them directly to stop.
